## Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions require a [DCO](https://developercertificate.org/) sign-off:
add a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line to each commit
(`git commit -s`). By signing off you certify that you wrote the change or
otherwise have the right to submit it under the project's licenses.
